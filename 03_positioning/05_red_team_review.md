# BriefPilot v2 Red-Team Review

> **Labeling rules** (used throughout the project): **[Fact]** has a source URL and the original page was opened; **[Summary]** based only on a search snippet or secondhand account; **[Inference]** derived from facts; **[Assumption · to be validated]** requires interviews or experiments. All external sources were accessed at the time of writing. WebFetch returns quotes produced by a summarization model, so the English quotes below **should be checked against the original page verbatim; reopen the page before citing them**.
>
> **Roles**: I play two people. One is the most demanding client, who asks, "Feishu, Otter, and Fireflies already do this, so why are you building it?" The other is a big-tech AI PM interviewer, who will open competitor websites on the spot and check your claims sentence by sentence.
>
> **Overall assessment (the good news first)**: The self-critique in these documents is of a very high standard. Evidence levels are clearly labeled, kill lines are written down in advance, the simulated reviewers are explicitly labeled "not real people", and "zero interviews, zero real-world testing" is admitted repeatedly. So what follows is not about attitude but about **structural holes**: several claims do not match the project's own verification record (07); one input premise contradicts the product form; and the decision rule for the make-or-break hypothesis H1 is biased toward "keep going". If an interviewer catches these three, the whole narrative collapses.

| # | Problem | Why it is fatal | One-line fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| **X1** | **The description of Littlebird's evidence sources is inconsistent with 07 L3 and with the official website.** The positioning, PRD, and Q&A notes Q3 all say Littlebird's "evidence sources are email and documents".
